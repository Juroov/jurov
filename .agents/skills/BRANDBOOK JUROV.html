VOICE
Chapter 03 · Typography

Three faces, one voice.

Aa Bb Cc
Regular Bold Black Italic
PLAYFAIR DISPLAY — Display & heading face. High-contrast serif for headlines; italic reserved for the single word that carries the emotional weight of the line.
0123 456+ 789
BEBAS NEUE — Reserved exclusively for numerals: statistics, counters, and impact figures. Never used for prose.
Protection · Education · Growth · Security
MONTSERRAT — UI labels, navigation, badges (uppercase, 0.3em tracking) and body copy (sentence case, regular weight).
  • Italic is a spotlight, not a style — use it on one phrase per headline, never the whole line.
  • Uppercase tracking never drops below 0.25em; tight caps read as generic UI, not this brand.
  • Body copy stays Ash, never full white — full-contrast white is reserved for headings only.
MARK
Chapter 04 · Logo & Mark

A shield, drawn once.

Kuya Juan.
Clearspace — keep an empty margin equal to the shield's own width on every side; nothing else enters that zone.

Minimum size — 28px tall in digital contexts; below that, use the shield mark alone without the wordmark.

Never — recolor the shield, rotate it, or set the wordmark in anything but Playfair Display Italic.
